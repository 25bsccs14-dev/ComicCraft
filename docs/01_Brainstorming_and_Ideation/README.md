# Phase 1: Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title
ComicCraft – AI Comic Story Creator

## Problem Statement
Creating a comic typically requires a person to develop a story, organize it into scenes, and create suitable illustrations. Beginners may find it difficult to turn a simple idea into a structured comic.

## Proposed Solution
ComicCraft is a web-based application that accepts a user's story idea and uses AI services to generate a short comic-style story and an accompanying illustration.

## Objectives
- Accept a user's creative idea through a simple web interface.
- Generate a comic story divided into four panels.
- Generate a comic-style illustration based on the user's idea.
- Display the generated content on a webpage.

## Intended Users
- Students and beginners interested in storytelling.
- Comic and creative-writing enthusiasts.
- Users who want help developing an initial comic idea.

## Core Features
1. Story idea input.
2. AI-generated four-panel story.
3. AI-generated illustration.
4. Display of results in the browser.

## Expected Benefits
- Supports creative brainstorming.
- Makes experimenting with comic ideas easier.
- Demonstrates how web development and AI services can be integrated.

## Initial Scope
The current project focuses on generating a short story and one illustration from a user-provided idea. Multi-page comic books, user accounts, and advanced editing are outside the current scope unless implemented later.
